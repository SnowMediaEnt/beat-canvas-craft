# Math: Lowest Common Denominator (LCD)

**Class date:** Oct 12, 2026 ([slide photo](../images/2026-10-12-lowest-common-denominator.jpg))

## Why it matters
You can only add or subtract fractions when they have the **same bottom number** (denominator). The LCD is the smallest number every denominator divides into evenly.

## Slide example
**Problem:** 1/16 + 2/3 + 1/12 + 5/9 + 3/8 = ?

### Steps 1–5: Find the LCD with the "division table"
Write all the denominators in a row. Divide by a number that goes into **at least two** of them. Numbers it doesn't divide into just drop down unchanged. Repeat until every number is 1.

| Divide by | 16 | 3 | 12 | 9 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | 4 | 3 | 3 | 9 | 2 |
| **3** | 4 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| **2** | 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| **2** | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| **3** | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

### Step 6: Multiply the divisors
4 × 3 × 2 × 2 × 3 = **144** ← the LCD

### Step 7: Convert each fraction to /144
Ask "what do I multiply the bottom by to get 144?" and multiply the top by the same number.

| Fraction | × | New fraction |
|---|---|---|
| 1/16 | × 9 | 9/144 |
| 2/3 | × 48 | 96/144 |
| 1/12 | × 12 | 12/144 |
| 5/9 | × 16 | 80/144 |
| 3/8 | × 18 | 54/144 |

Add the tops: 9 + 96 + 12 + 80 + 54 = **251**, so the sum is **251/144**.

### Step 8: Simplify to a mixed number
251 ÷ 144 = 1 with 107 left over, so the answer is **1 107/144**.

## Calculator check (TI-30Xa)
1 `a b/c` 16 `+` 2 `a b/c` 3 `+` 1 `a b/c` 12 `+` 5 `a b/c` 9 `+` 3 `a b/c` 8 `=` → shows **1 ⌟ 107 ⌟ 144**

Doing it by hand is how you learn it; the calculator is how you check it.
