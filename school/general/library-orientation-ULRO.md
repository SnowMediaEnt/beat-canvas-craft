# Library Orientation (ULRO): TO DO ASAP

**Received:** Oct 12, 2026 (letter from the CAU librarian)
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

## What it is
The **Undergraduate Learning Resource Orientation (ULRO)**. It teaches you how to use the CAU **LIRN databases** and the **Virtual Library**, and covers APA formatting and avoiding plagiarism.

## How to complete it
1. Accept the invitation sent to your **student email**.
2. Open **Canvas**, go to your dashboard, and select the **ULRO** course.
3. Complete each item in order. Each one unlocks the next, starting with "Welcome to the Learning Resource Orientation Center! (START HERE)".
4. Finish every required section **before** the final quiz.
5. **You must complete question 2 in the quiz to earn the certificate.**
6. The librarian emails you a **Certificate of Completion** at your student email.

**Time needed:** about 30 to 45 minutes.

## Why it matters
- **Every assignment has a 20-point APA section, even discussion questions.** Correct APA formatting can nearly guarantee those points.
- About 75% of CAU coursework is academic.
- The librarian and academic staff will keep contacting you until the quiz is done.
- Recommended in your first few weeks, since each assignment builds on earlier work.
