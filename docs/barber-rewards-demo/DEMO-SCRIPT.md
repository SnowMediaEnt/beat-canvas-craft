# Barber Rewards — how to demo it

Open **https://id-preview--a71b9a1b-6b51-481c-9f50-45af36874336.lovable.app**

The landing page has two buttons — **Enter as a Customer** and **Enter as the Barber** —
so there is no signup to sit through. Once inside, the **Demo pill in the header**
flips between the two sides, which is how you show both perspectives without
logging in and out.

Best on a phone. If you have two phones, put the customer on one and the barber on
the other and do the scan for real — that is the moment that sells it.

## The five-minute walkthrough

**1. Open as the customer.** Home shows his 740 points and a progress bar — *4 more
cuts to a free haircut*. That bar is the hook: it gives him a reason to come back to
this shop instead of the one down the street.

**2. Tap My Card.** Big QR code and the 6-digit member code. This is the screen the
client holds up at the chair. Nothing to carry, nothing to lose.

**3. Flip to the Barber side** using the Demo pill, and tap **Check out a client**.
Type **620392** (or scan the customer's QR with the camera if you have two phones).
Marcus comes up with his balance and visit count.

**4. Tap two services** — say *Skin Fade* and *Beard Trim & Shape*. Watch the point
and dollar totals add up live. Hit confirm. Big "+180 points" confirmation.

> Tell the barber: it takes about four seconds at the end of a cut. That's the whole
> ask. No paperwork, no punch cards to reprint, nothing for him to remember.

**5. Flip back to the customer.** The balance went up, and the new visit is in
History with the services listed. Same in reverse — it's one shared system.

**6. Redeem something.** Go to Rewards, cash in a reward, and a code appears under
My Codes. Marcus already has one waiting: **HDWAXR** for a Free Line-Up.

**7. Flip to the barber, Checkout → Redeem tab**, enter **HDWAXR**, and mark it
fulfilled. Try it a second time — it refuses. Codes can't be double-spent, so no
one can screenshot a reward and reuse it.

## Then show him it's his shop, not a template

- **Settings** — his shop name is the very first field. Type his real shop name in
  front of him and let him watch it change everywhere. This lands every time.
- **Services** — his menu, his prices, and how many points each service earns. He
  controls the whole economy.
- **Clients** — every client with visit history and **private cut notes** only he
  can see ("#2 on the sides, skin taper, scissors on top"). Point out the **Lapsed**
  filter: clients who haven't been in for weeks, with a text button right there.
  That filter is money — it's the difference between hoping they come back and
  actually asking.
- **Promotions** — "Slow Tuesday: Double Points" is already set up. Explain how he
  can use points to push people into his dead hours instead of discounting.
- **Rewards** — free cuts and discounts, but also perks that cost him nothing:
  skip-the-line priority booking, bring-a-kid-free.

## Worth saying out loud

- It installs to the home screen like an app — no App Store, no download friction.
- Points can only be issued by him, from his logged-in account. A client can't add
  their own points, and can't see what he's written about them.
- Everything in here is fake sample data — three made-up barbers and eight made-up
  clients — so nothing shown is a real person.
- No name, logo, or colors are locked in yet. This is the skeleton; branding comes
  after he says yes.

## If someone asks "what's missing?"

Honest answer: booking is in here and works, but if he already runs Booksy or
Squire, the smart move is to keep his existing booking and just point this at
loyalty. Text and email notifications aren't wired to a provider yet, and there's
no payment processing — prices are for point math and display only.
